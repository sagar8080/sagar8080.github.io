02 / What it does

Six principles, one workbench.

Kivi is being built around the full lifecycle of delegated work. These principles guide the product and the capabilities being developed.

01

Start with an objective

Define the outcome, acceptance criteria, and boundaries before delegating. The work stays anchored to what you asked for.

02

Keep the work connected

The design carries plans, approvals, execution records, and evidence across conversations, model changes, and interruptions.

03

Set explicit boundaries

Permissions, data disclosure, and cost limits govern execution. Sensitive actions require the right approval.

04

Work across disciplines

Bring analytics, pipelines, applied ML, AI tools, and operations into the same project and review process.

05

Verify the outcome

Connect checks to acceptance criteria and retain the evidence needed to judge whether the objective was met.

06

Build for recovery

Preserve waiting work and reconcile uncertain operations before retrying, so an interruption does not mean starting over.

03 / Where it fits

Where it earns its keep.

The product spans data, AI, and ML workflows where a useful answer needs to become inspectable, executable work.

Data investigation

Investigate a metric change, inspect the relevant data, and keep the queries and evidence alongside the diagnosis.

Engineering changes

Propose a pipeline or model change, review the diff, validate it, and prepare the work for release within agreed limits.

Applied ML

Connect feature work, training, evaluation, and prediction to a clear objective and inspectable results.

Operational follow-through

Carry investigations into cost, failed runs, and incidents through proposed fixes and verification, with approvals along the way.
